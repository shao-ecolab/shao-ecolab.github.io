# Research

We build process-based ecosystem models that treat microorganisms as active participants in biogeochemical cycling, and test them against field and laboratory data across peatlands, forests, and croplands.

## Research Areas

- Plant–microbe interactions and mycorrhizal ecology
- Process-based ecosystem and biogeochemical modeling
- Peatland carbon and nutrient cycling
- Enhanced rock weathering and soil-based carbon dioxide removal
- Microbial trait representation in Earth system models
- Knowledge-guided machine learning and ecosystem digital twins
