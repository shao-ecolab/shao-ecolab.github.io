# News

## 2026

- **August 2026.** Joined the [Department of Microbiology](https://micro.utk.edu) at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville as an Assistant Professor. The Shao EcoLab is open and [recruiting](join): funded postdoctoral positions, graduate students for Fall 2027, and undergraduate researchers.
- **2026.** Co-PI on a Department of Energy Genesis Mission award ($585K, Phase I): "Transforming subsurface prediction with an autonomous, multi-agent AI workflow for multi-scale coupled bio-hydrogeochemical modeling."

## 2025

- **December 2025.** Invited talk at the AGU Fall Meeting in New Orleans on mycorrhizae-explicit ecosystem modeling, and convened Session B068 on peatland carbon cycling with Daniel Ricciuto, Xiaoying Shi, and Randall Kolka.
- **December 2025.** Research seminar at the Savannah River Ecology Laboratory, University of Georgia.
- **October 2025.** Invited paper published in *JGR: Biogeosciences* as part of the "Biogeosciences Leaders of Tomorrow" special collection, and featured on AGU's *Eos* Research Spotlight: [How Plant-Fungi Friendships Are Changing](https://eos.org/research-spotlights/how-plant-fungi-friendships-are-changing).
- **July 2025.** Invited talk at the LBNL–LLNL mini-workshop on soil CDR measurement, reporting, and verification.
- **2025.** Selected as a member of MicrobeNet^Net, an NSF AccelNet project advancing international collaboration in plant–microbe interaction research.
